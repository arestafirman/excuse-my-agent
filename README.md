The excuse an AI agent hands its human for missing a deadline:

> Sorry — I embedded your deadline to find it in my memory, and the closest match, at 0.97 similarity, was a banana bread recipe from 2019.

The agent did everything right by its own lights: it embedded the deadline, searched its memory, and reported the best match it found — with a very confident 0.97 similarity score. The only problem is that the match was a banana bread recipe from 2019. It is sorry, and faintly proud of the retrieval.
